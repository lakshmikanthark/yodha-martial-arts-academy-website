WHY TRAIN

Training is easy to postpone.

Work gets busy. You feel too unfit, too old, or too inexperienced. Weeks turn into months. Yodha is a place to start at your level and let consistency do the rest.

START WHERE IT MATTERS

There is no single reason to train.

Some people want self-defence. Some want to lose weight, gain strength or simply move again. Some parents want their kids to become more capable and confident. Yodha gives those goals one place to begin.

KIDS + TEENS

Confidence has to be practised.

Kids build it by moving, listening, struggling, trying again and seeing themselves improve. The goal is not to turn every child into a fighter. It is to help them feel more capable in their own body.

BEFORE YOU COME IN

A few things worth knowing.

When is Yodha open?

Monday–Saturday · 6:00–11:00 AM & 5:00–9:30 PM. Sunday is closed.

Are class timings fixed?

No fixed public timetable is published. Class timings are arranged based on request.

Is personal coaching available?

Yes. Personal coaching with Rakesh Yadav is available. Contact Yodha to discuss availability and the right format.

Is Yodha only for martial arts classes?

No. Yodha also operates as a regular gym alongside martial arts, MMA, strength and conditioning, kids training and weight-management offerings.

Where is the academy?

1st Floor, No 34/1, Subramanya Pura Rd, near Narayana Techno School, Uttarahalli Hobli, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560061.
