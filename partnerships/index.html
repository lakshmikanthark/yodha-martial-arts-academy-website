Self Defence Workshops

For institutions, the supplied program focuses on practical self-defence, unarmed-combat basics and situational awareness, helping students and staff build safety awareness, physical fitness, mental alertness, self-discipline and confidence.

Kids Workout Workshops

Sessions at the academy and in residential societies combine playful fitness routines with basic martial-arts movement to build strength, flexibility, focus, teamwork and confidence while keeping children active in a safe, age-appropriate and motivating environment.

POLICE + SECURITY TRAINING

Self-defence and unarmed combat for field readiness.

Yodha’s supplied program material describes specialized training for police personnel focused on reflexes, tactical awareness, close-combat efficiency, practical defence scenarios, stress-response conditioning, control and safety in high-pressure situations. The archive also includes Quick Response Team training for Bengaluru City Police and CRPF armed and unarmed training.
