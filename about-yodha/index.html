FOUNDER · RAKESH YADAV

A life built around martial arts, fitness and coaching.

Rakesh began training at 13 and later founded Yodha School of Martial Arts in 2005. His background spans Muay Thai, Kickboxing, Gymnastics, Karate, Bulwark Training, Boxing and Jiu-Jitsu.

01

Martial arts career

Rakesh’s supplied profile records more than 150 professional fights and over 100 victories, along with gold medals and a world champion title. It also describes work choreographing action sequences for South Indian cinema and years spent coaching and mentoring martial artists, athletes and fitness enthusiasts.

02

Fitness & bodybuilding

His fitness background spans more than two decades. The supplied profile lists him as a certified fitness trainer, CrossFit expert, strength & conditioning specialist and certified nutritionist, with work around fat loss, inch reduction and muscle building. His own bodybuilding journey began after being bullied for a lean physique and later led to amateur bodybuilding competition wins.

03

Professional life

The profile provided for this site lists M.Sc, MBA and LLB qualifications, more than 12 years of experience as a Business Analyst in the IT industry, and a Sports President role within a political organization connected with sports development and youth empowerment.
